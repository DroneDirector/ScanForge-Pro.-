# ScanForge Pro 1.1.2 (alpha)

## Live Scan fixes (from the first real test)
- **Selects only the object you tap.** The table height is measured, and the object region stays tight around what you tapped. The table and things behind it (like a cup) are no longer included.
- **Hologram only on the object.** The dots now build up on the object itself, without the red "rain" over the table.
- **The percentage never goes down** while you scan.
- **Dots turn green** where your photos have covered them.
- **What you captured stays visible.** The hologram stays when tracking briefly drops, and it's still there, in the same place, when you look away and come back.
- **Easier to take photos.** It's less strict about holding still and framing, so photos come faster as you move around.
- *Point the camera at the object* only shows when the object really is out of view.
- *Detail %* appears once enough of the object is captured.

Update from inside the app; your scans and settings are kept.
