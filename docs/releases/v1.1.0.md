# ScanForge Pro 1.1.0 (alpha): Live Scan

## New: Live Scan
*New scan → Live Scan*
- **Tap the object.** An on-device AI outlines it (cyan glow), and the app knows what to scan.
- **Walk around it.** Sharp photos are taken automatically at every new angle, with the exact camera position from ARCore.
- **Watch the hologram build.** The object's measured surface glows over the camera view:
  - **green** = captured, **yellow** = seen in one photo, **red** = still needed
  - rings around the object light up for each angle you've covered
- **Captured % and Detail %** show how complete and how sharp the scan is. Hints tell you where to go next (walk around, go higher, fill the red spots).
- **Done → Build.** The model comes out at its **real size** in millimetres. No measuring needed.
- **Plain and dark objects** (like a black gamepad) now work much better, because the AI outline of every photo cuts away the background.

## New: GPS for large scans
*Settings → Location, off by default*
- Uses GPS data in imported photos (e.g. DJI drone photos) and the phone's location when taking photos.
- Gives buildings, terrain and drone scans real size and a north-up orientation.
- Used only for building the model and kept on your phone; never added to exported files.

## Requirements
- Live Scan needs a phone that supports Google Play Services for AR (ARCore). The app offers to install it if needed.
- Phones without ARCore can still use *Scan with camera* and *Import photos*.

## Fixed
- The "Normal" quality label no longer wraps.

Update from inside the app; your scans and settings are kept.
