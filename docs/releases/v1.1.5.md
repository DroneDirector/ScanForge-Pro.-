# ScanForge Pro 1.1.5 (alpha)

## Better AI detection of the object
- **Zoomed AI detection.** The AI first finds roughly where the object is, then looks again at a full-resolution close-up of that area. Far-away and small objects get a sharp, complete outline instead of a soft blob.
- **Edges that snap to the photo.** The outline is aligned to the real edges in the picture, so edges and corners are clean.

## Crisper 3D models
- **Edges, corners and slopes stay sharp.** Edge-preserving smoothing replaces the even smoothing that rounded corners off. Flat and curved areas are still smoothed.

Update from inside the app; your scans and settings are kept.
