# ScanForge Pro 1.2.0 (alpha): Scan box and Turntable

Choose how to scan before the camera opens: **New scan → Live Scan (AR) / Turntable / Scan with camera / Import photos**.

## Live Scan: scan box and dome
1. **Tap the object.** A 3D box is placed around it, fitted automatically by the AI (or from depth with *AI guess* off).
2. **Fit the box.** Sliders for width, depth, height and rotation. Tap the table to move the box. *Reselect* starts over.
3. **Start scan** and walk around. A **dome** of segments around the box turns **green** for every direction you've photographed: low, middle and from above.
4. The **percentage follows the dome**, so it never jumps or drops, even if you look away.
5. *Dots* shows the optional surface dots on top.
6. The 3D engine uses the box as the exact object region, which gives a cleaner model and a flat base.

## Turntable (new)
- Phone on a stand or tripod. The object turns on a turntable or lazy Susan (a newspaper or patterned paper on it helps).
- A photo every **1, 1.5, 2 or 3 seconds**, **24, 36 or 48** per round.
- Up to **three rounds**: level with the object, a bit higher, and from above. The app guides you between rounds.
- Focus and exposure are locked during each round, so all photos match.
- When you finish, the **AI separates the object from the background** in every photo. The 3D engine then uses only the turning part of the photos, so the still background doesn't confuse it.
- Best for small objects like a computer mouse.

Update from inside the app; your scans and settings are kept.
