# ScanForge Pro 1.0.4 (alpha)

## New
- **Latest updates** drop-down in Settings → Updates:
  - Shows the 3 newest versions and what each one changed.
  - The newest is marked **Latest**, the one on your phone **Installed**.
  - When a newer version exists, tap **Update to …** right there.
- **Cancel** an update while it downloads.

## Unchanged
- The app still checks for updates at start-up. You can choose *Later*, *Skip this version*, or turn the check off in Settings.
- Your scans and settings are kept when updating.
