# ScanForge Pro 1.3.0 (alpha): Auto scan

**New scan → Auto scan.** No points to collect and nothing to tap: just move around the object.

## Auto scan
- The phone's depth builds the object's shape **live** while you move. Height and distance can change all the time; AR tracking places every measurement, and close passes add detail.
- Sharp photos are taken automatically, with the phone's position and depth saved for each one. They paint the **real colours** onto the shape.
- A **glowing light sweep** runs over the surface you're scanning.
- Coverage colours: **red** = not scanned yet, **yellow** = partly, **green** = done, **real colours** = fully captured.
- A percentage that never drops, and hints that point to what's missing ("Move to the left around the object", "Raise the phone and look down at the top", "Move lower to scan underneath").
- Needs a phone where ARCore can measure depth.

## Remove the surface underneath (on/off)
- Cuts away the table or floor the object stands on and closes the bottom, so only the object is left. Off keeps a thin slab of the surface as a base.
- Switch it before scanning (the surface shows faded in the live view) or on the project screen afterwards, with no rescan. Default in **Settings → Auto scan**.

## Flip & scan bottom
- After the first model is built, tap **Flip & scan bottom**, turn the object over and scan again.
- Both scans are lined up automatically and combined into **one complete, closed model**, bottom included.
- Any two scans of the same object can also be combined with **Combine with another scan…**.

## 3D engine
- Uses the phone's depth together with the photos, so plain objects with little texture still get their shape. Edges and corners stay sharp.
