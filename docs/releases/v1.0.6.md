# ScanForge Pro 1.0.6 (alpha)

## Changed
- **You decide when there are too few photos.** The app no longer stops the build on its own.
  - It warns: *Too few overlapping photos detected… Do you still want to proceed?*
  - Choose **Proceed** to build the model anyway, or **Stop** to add more photos first.
- It asks at most at three moments:
  - before building (fewer than 20 photos)
  - after placing the photos (too few could be placed)
  - when the surface is checked (too little was measured)
- Once you choose *Proceed*, it won't ask again for that build.
- Only scans with fewer than 3 photos can't be built at all.

Update from inside the app; your scans and settings are kept.
