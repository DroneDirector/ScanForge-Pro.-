# ScanForge Pro 1.0.1 (alpha)

## New
- **Animated start screen** in the style of the app icon: a point cloud gathers into the cube, an orange scan line sweeps over it, the scan rings spin up, and a glowing progress bar shows the app getting ready (engine, your scans, viewer).
- The start-up background now matches the app, so there's no black flash.

Everything from 1.0.0 is unchanged. Install over the previous version; your scans are kept.
