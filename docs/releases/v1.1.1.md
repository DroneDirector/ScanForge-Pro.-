# ScanForge Pro 1.1.1 (alpha)

## Easier scanning of small objects
- **Sharper small objects.** When measuring depth, the 3D engine now zooms in on the object in every photo, so a small object keeps its detail instead of turning into a blob. This works in all scan modes.
- **Live Scan for small things.**
  - Tapping works even when the phone's depth sensing is too coarse to see the object (it's sized from the AI outline).
  - The AI outline in each photo shapes the hologram, so small objects light up too.
  - Photos are taken from as close as 10 cm.
  - Hints tell you the right distance (about 15–25 cm for small objects).
- **Tap to focus** in *Scan with camera*, so the camera focuses on the object and not the background.
- **Cleaner flat base**: leftovers of the table around the bottom are removed.

## Tips for small objects
- Put the object on a newspaper or patterned cloth. AR tracking needs some detail around it.
- Use good, even light.
- Hold the phone about 15–25 cm away and move slowly.

Update from inside the app; your scans and settings are kept.
