# ScanForge Pro 1.1.3 (alpha)

## Live Scan takes photos reliably
- **A photo every few centimetres** (or a few degrees of turning) as you move around the object. Steps are smaller for small objects.
- **Only blurry pictures are skipped.** The old rules needed steadiness, framing and size estimates to all be right at once, and that blocked too many photos.
- **Starts right away**, even before you tap the object. If you don't tap, the object in the middle of the screen is picked after a moment.
- **The hint tells you why** when photos aren't being taken: *Move a little slower*, *Hold steady for a moment*, *Keep the object in view*.
- **Diagnostics:** Live Scan writes a short log to the scan's log (log button on the scan page), so problems can be tracked down.

Update from inside the app; your scans and settings are kept.
