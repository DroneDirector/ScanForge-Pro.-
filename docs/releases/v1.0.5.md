# ScanForge Pro 1.0.5 (alpha)

## Fixed
- **No more box-shaped models from too few photos.**
  - The app now checks every scan: how many photos could be placed around the object, and how much of its surface was actually measured.
  - If it isn't enough, the build stops with a clear **Not enough photos** message and tips, instead of making a block.

## New
- **Warning before building** a scan with fewer than 20 photos: choose *Take more photos* or *Build anyway*.
- **Take more photos** button right on the error message.

## Tip
For good results take 30–60 photos all the way around the object, plus a second round from higher up.
