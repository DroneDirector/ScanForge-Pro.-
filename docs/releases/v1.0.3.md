# ScanForge Pro 1.0.3 (alpha)

## New
- **Bambu Lab P2S** (256 × 256 × 256 mm) and **Bambu Lab H2S** (340 × 320 × 340 mm) added to the printer list.
  - Pick them in the export sheet or as the default printer in Settings.
  - The 3MF is centred on the right build plate and the app warns if the model is too big.

Update from inside the app; your scans and settings are kept.
